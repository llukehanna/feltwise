Feltwise Privacy Policy

Effective: 10 November 2026

Feltwise is a blackjack training app for iPhone. It does not collect, store off-device, share or sell any personal information.

  • No data leaves your device. Your training history and settings are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Feltwise has no servers. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; Feltwise never sees your payment details. The app makes no other network requests. The Wizard of Odds link in Settings, and the Terms and Privacy links on the Feltwise Pro screen, open in Safari.
  • No accounts. You never sign in or give us your name, email or any other details.
  • No tracking, ads or analytics. Feltwise contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code.
  • Deleting your data. Settings → Reset progress deletes your training history. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.
  • Children. Feltwise is not directed at children.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
